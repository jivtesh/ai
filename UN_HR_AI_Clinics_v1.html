Be specific about context: "For a P-4 in a field mission" beats "for a job." Start simple. Follow up to refine.

📱 Universal HR Catch-Up Prompts Works everywhere

💡 Memorize these 5: They work in almost any HR situation.

Prompt When to Use
"What did I miss this week?" Monday morning / returning from leave
"Summarize this" Works on policies, emails, meeting notes
"What's being asked of me?" Unclear requests, long email threads
"Draft a professional response" Grievances, candidate emails, policy queries
"Make this shorter and clearer" Any HR communication before sending

🧭 The capability split: which Copilot do you need?

There are two useful tiers. 💬 Chat (free) is web-grounded and works when you paste or upload content into chat. 🟣 M365 Copilot (paid) adds embedded help inside Outlook, Teams, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and work-grounded chat across your Microsoft 365 content.

💬 Chat (free)

  • Web search and general reasoning
  • Draft, rewrite, and summarise text you paste in
  • Compare two uploaded documents
  • Analyse an uploaded spreadsheet
  • Generate images, talking points, and social posts
  • Answer policy questions from documents you paste in
  • Enterprise Data Protection when signed in with a work account
Cannot: automatically read your inbox, calendar, Teams chats, or SharePoint files; summarise meetings without a transcript; or work as an embedded assistant inside Office apps.

🟣 M365 Copilot (paid)

  • Outlook inbox triage, thread summaries, and grounded replies
  • Teams real-time recap, action items, decisions, and intelligent recap
  • Excel workbook analysis, formulas, PivotTables, and data cleanup in place
  • Word drafting and rewriting grounded in files
  • PowerPoint decks from a document or prompt
  • Work-grounded chat across email, files, chats, and meetings
  • Create Agents for recurring workflows

📋 How to Use This Guide

  1. Pick a category relevant to your role today.
  2. Check the tier badge: free Chat works when you paste or upload; paid M365 Copilot works inside Microsoft 365 apps.
  3. Start with scaffolded prompts in yellow boxes if you are new to Copilot.

# Image prompts for the background plates

One prompt per plate. Generate at 1920x1080 (or larger, same ratio), export
as webp, and drop the file over the matching path under `public/scenes/`.
No code changes needed. Shared constraints for every prompt: no text, no
logos, no flags, no real maps or borders, no recognizable people. Palette
anchored to deep ink blue `#0B0F16`, warm gold `#D9A441`, signal blue
`#2E9BD6`. Cinematic, quiet, museum-at-night mood. Strong single key light,
deep shadows, soft film grain, gentle vignette. The plate sits behind
bright UI, so keep the upper left quadrant calm and avoid hot highlights
in the center.

## public/scenes/attract/bg.webp

A darkened modern gallery corridor at night, one point perspective, empty,
five tall doorways receding into darkness, each leaking a faint cold blue
glow onto a polished dark floor, two soft cones of light falling from
unseen ceiling fixtures with fine dust suspended in them, near black ink
blue palette with restrained blue accents, cinematic, atmospheric, no
text, no logos, no people.

## public/scenes/legal/bg.webp

A vast dim law library at night rendered as soft silhouette, tall
bookshelf stacks receding, one arched window admitting pale cool light,
faint blue glints along book spines, deep shadows, ink blue palette with
one restrained cool highlight on the right third, quiet and monumental,
out of focus background suitable behind interface text, no text, no
logos, no people.

## public/scenes/procurement/bg.webp

ink blue darkness with amber accents on the right, calm and orderly, soft
haze, no text, no logos, no people.

## public/scenes/resident-coordinator/bg.webp
